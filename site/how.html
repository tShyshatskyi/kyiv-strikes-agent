Agents judge. Code checks.

After a night attack on Kyiv, a dozen channels report the same hits in different words, at different hours, with casualty figures that change through the morning. Counting posts counts each strike five times. Reading them by hand takes a person a day per night.

This registry hands the reading to three AI agents with separate jobs, one of which exists only to find the others' mistakes. Everything that can be checked without judgement is checked by code, so an agent's error is caught by something that cannot make the same error.

      Limits

      1. Reports are what they are

        Telegram posts are early and sometimes corrected later. Casualty figures are the latest a source gave, not a final count.

      2. Many places have no address

        Officials usually name a district, not a street, for security reasons. Those places are drawn as rings at a fixed point inside their district.

      3. The auditor is a model too

        It catches a lot, but what it still disputes after one revision is published with its note attached rather than silently fixed.

      4. Wikipedia is not a witness

        It retells the same news, so it never raises a place's confirmation. It is used only to check whole nights and death tolls.
